# Product Decisions

## Decision 001

### Question
Should users choose from predefined emotion descriptions?

### Decision
No.

### Reason
People experience emotions differently. Providing a fixed sentence like "I feel optimistic today" may unintentionally limit self-expression.

Instead, users should:
- choose a broad emotion (Happy, Sad, Calm...)
- describe it in their own words
- receive AI-assisted suggestions only if they need help expressing themselves.

### Future Enhancement
Echo will eventually analyze free-text input and suggest emotions rather than forcing users into predefined labels.
